# Caring for Children

**Find the child's world and communicate with them in that world.**

An adult-facing course in Essential Knowledge for Life for parents, guardians and other caregivers. Learn to understand what a child means, respect thoughts and feelings, join curiosity and play, and keep kind boundaries. Do not expect adult reasoning or emotional control from a child. Joining their world does not remove your responsibility for care and safety.

## Start here

Read in order or use the lesson list. No installation, AI account, purchases or real-child participation is required. Examples are fictional. You may pause or skip an upsetting scenario without explaining why. The [caregiver guide](CAREGIVER-GUIDE.md) explains how to study alone or with other adults.

This is general education, not diagnosis, treatment, a childcare qualification or an individualized safety plan. It has not been tested for learning effectiveness with families. For immediate danger use local emergency help; for specific health, developmental or protection concerns use appropriate professional services. [Safety and help](lessons/11-safety.md).

## Age and individual needs

The reader is an adult. Examples span infancy, childhood and adolescence; they are not instructions for children to manage adults. Age is a starting point, not a uniform ability test. Use the child's established communication supports. Do not force eye contact, affection or disclosure. Infant sleep and feeding need distinct guidance; an older child's routine is not suitable by default. See [communication](lessons/03-language.md) and [daily care](lessons/08-care.md).
