# Sources and scope

[Course home](README.md)

Checked 12 September 2026. Sources support the limited points below, not every fictional dialogue or a claim that the course has been clinically tested. Exercises, tables and communication frameworks are authored teaching examples. Source organizations have not endorsed this course. Links are references, not incorporated third-party licensed content.

| Source | Use and limit |
|---|---|
| [UNICEF: communicating with young children](https://www.unicef.org/eca/stories/how-communicate-effectively-your-young-child) | Listening and clear language in lessons 1 and 3. Our examples allow alternatives to eye contact/touch. |
| [CPS: positive discipline](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/behavior-and-development/positive-discipline-for-young-children) | Feelings, appropriate expectations and avoiding physical punishment/shaming in lessons 4 and 6. No time-out protocol reproduced. |
| [CPS: feeding a picky eater](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/healthy-living/when_your_child_is_a_picky_eater) | General meal context in lesson 8; not infant feeding or individualized therapy. |
| [CPS: healthy teeth](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/healthy-living/healthy_teeth_for_children) | Help with brushing; no toothpaste dosage or dental treatment specified. |
| [CPS: healthy sleep](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/healthy-living/healthy_sleep_for_your_baby_and_child) | Age matters and routines; no universal sleep duration prescribed. |
| [Health Canada: safe sleep](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/safe-sleep/safe-sleep-tips.html) | Brief infant sleep boundary, lesson 8. Read complete guidance; summary is not exhaustive. |
| [CDC: developmental concerns](https://www.cdc.gov/concerned) | Talk to a health professional about concerns, lesson 10. US service pathways do not automatically apply in Canada. |
| [Ontario: reporting responsibilities](https://files.ontario.ca/pdf-3/mccss-report-child-abuse-and-neglect-en-2022-03-31.pdf) | Ontario-specific reporting distinction in lesson 11. Official PDF used because the HTML page returned access denied during review. |
| [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) | Exact reuse terms; the plain-language licence notice is a summary. |

No developmental milestone schedule, diagnostic screening instrument, medication advice or individualized safeguarding plan is provided. Medical and legal guidance can change; recheck the relevant official sources when using it for a real decision. Immediate danger requires local emergency services, not a course response.

[Government of Canada emergency-help guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/campaigns/get-help-here.html) supports the Canadian emergency number in lesson 11.
