# Glossary

[Course home](README.md)

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Perspective | How a situation may look to someone; ask rather than assume. |
| Observation | Something actually seen or heard, separate from its possible explanation. |
| Interpretation | A proposed meaning, which may be wrong. |
| Tentative language | Wording that leaves room for correction, such as “You seem…” |
| Acknowledgment | Recognizing an experience without necessarily agreeing with an action. |
| Boundary | A clear limit the adult is responsible for carrying out appropriately. |
| Real choice | Options that are both feasible and acceptable within the limit. |
| Repair | Taking responsibility for a mistake and changing what you do next. |
| Autonomy | Having a voice and appropriate choices; it does not erase adult care duties. |
| Privacy | Appropriate control and care over personal information and space. |
| Communication support | A method or aid that helps a particular person understand or express themselves. |
| Developmental concern | A reason to seek appropriate guidance, not a diagnosis made by this course. |
| Safeguarding | Protecting children from harm through appropriate actions and services. |
| Fictional example | An invented situation, not a report about a real family. |

These plain-language explanations support the lessons; they are not statutory or clinical definitions.
