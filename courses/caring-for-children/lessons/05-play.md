# 5. Connect through play and everyday moments

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Join an interest without turning it into an adult performance test.

## Explanation

Ask what role, if any, you can have. A child may want you to watch, participate, provide materials, or leave their activity alone. “What happens next in your story?” can invite imagination; repeated corrections about realism may end the story you hoped to share.

Connection need not involve new purchases, screens or scheduled lessons. A made-up story during a walk can be enough. Choose activities appropriate to the child's abilities and safe environment. Small objects, water, climbing and tools need specific safety consideration; an imaginary game does not remove real hazards.

Our examples are optional editorial activities. Their value is the opportunity to understand each other, not evidence that a child becomes more intelligent or obedient. Stop if the activity is unwanted. Do not make play time conditional on an emotional disclosure.

## Worked example

Fictional: Deni declares a cardboard box a train. The adult asks, “May I be a passenger?” Deni wants a ticket collector instead. The adult follows that role. When the box is pushed toward the stairs, the adult moves the game to a safe area and explains the limit; the make-believe route can change.

## Practice

Fictional: a child is drawing an imaginary animal. Which invitations preserve the child's authorship? A. “It needs four legs; fix it.” B. “Would you like to tell me about it?” C. “I'll draw the correct version.” D. “Would you like company or quiet?” Identify a situation in which you would interrupt anyway.

## Answers and checking

B and D invite without taking over. A and C impose adult rules on harmless imagination. Interrupt a real hazard, such as unsafe use of a sharp tool. Explain the safety reason without declaring the imagined animal wrong.

## Try independently

Invent a no-purchase shared activity in a safe setting. State how the child can change the activity or stop.
