# 9. Respect autonomy and privacy

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Include the child in choices about their space, stories and body.

## Explanation

Ask before sharing a personal story or photograph, and be willing not to share. A child's agreement is not blanket permission for every audience or platform, nor proof that legal requirements are met. Adults remain responsible for risks the child cannot evaluate. A private conversation, service upload and public post are different actions.

Do not invite children to send this course their photos, names or family experiences. The supplied exercises work without real data. Avoid placing intimate, embarrassing or identifying details in public GitHub issues or AI tools.

For everyday interaction, explain necessary care and offer appropriate choices. No forced affection is needed to demonstrate politeness. Do not promise secrecy about safety; explain that you may need help from people whose job is to protect children. This is an ethical practice framework, not a statement that children of every age have identical legal decision-making powers.

## Worked example

Fictional: a child agrees to send a drawing to one grandparent. The adult then considers posting a photo showing the child's school uniform. The original agreement did not cover that. A better default is to keep it private and discuss the separate request without pressure; removing a name alone may not remove identifying details.

## Practice

Classify the permission in the example: A. one private recipient; B. any public account; C. an AI training service; D. permission to share all future drawings. What remains the adult's responsibility even for A?

## Answers and checking

Only A matches the stated agreement. The adult still checks the content, recipient and appropriate authority; agreement does not eliminate risk. B, C and D are separate uses. The child can change their mind before sending, and no upload is needed for learning here.

## Try independently

Draft an honest response to “Promise you won't tell anyone.” Offer listening without a secrecy promise or pressure to disclose.
