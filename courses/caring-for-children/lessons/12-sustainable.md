# 12. Build a sustainable caregiving approach

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Make a realistic plan that includes the child's voice and adult support.

## Explanation

A plan should fit actual time, responsibilities and resources. Decide who provides care, what must be handed over and how the child can express preferences. Do not assign a child the job of stabilizing an adult's emotions or resolving adult disputes.

Choose one everyday change, such as a clearer departure routine. Notice whether the child had a chance to express themselves and whether the adult kept the agreed boundary. Do not measure success only by silence or compliance. Revise an approach that is confusing or unwanted.

Caregivers also need practical support. Ask for appropriate help before exhaustion becomes a safety problem. This can mean a trusted safe adult, community support or a health professional. The course is not a substitute for childcare, respite or treatment.

Keep any personal reflections offline and minimal. Completion uses the fictional capstone below; no real child has to demonstrate a result.

![Ask; Make it feasible; Review](../images/voice.png)

Text equivalent: Ask: What would help?; An invitation, not a test. Make it feasible: Keep necessary boundaries; Adults arrange support. Review: What should change?; Compliance is not the score.

## Worked example

Fictional: two caregivers disagree about a morning routine. They agree privately on who prepares the bag and who supervises departure. They invite the child to choose between two feasible preparation options. If a choice repeatedly fails, they review the task rather than describe the child as the family's problem.

## Practice

Capstone: fictional Uma dislikes ending a drawing to leave. Leaving is necessary; the drawing can safely stay on the table. Write: one observation; one invitation; a clear boundary; two real choices; one repair if you shouted; and one way to review the approach.

## Answers and checking

Example: “You are still drawing. Would you like to tell me where you want to pause? We need to leave. It can stay here or go in the folder. I shouted earlier; I'm sorry. Later we can see whether a reminder or a different stopping point would help.” Check the folder really fits. Do not make Uma explain, forgive or stop feeling disappointed as a condition of care.

## Try independently

Use a new fictional scenario to check all six capstone elements. Explain what remains unknown and when outside help would be needed. Compare with the [curriculum completion guide](../CURRICULUM.md).
