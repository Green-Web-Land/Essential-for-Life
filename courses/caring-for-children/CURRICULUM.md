# Curriculum and completion

[Course home](README.md)

## Outcome

Use a child's perspective to improve an everyday interaction while preserving adult responsibility. Complete all twelve lessons and the supplied capstone; reviewing answer guidance matters more than speed. Take breaks as needed. No certificate or professional competence is conferred.

Lessons 1–5 practise understanding and connection; 6–7 boundaries and repair; 8–11 care, differences and safety; 12 integrates them. Each contains an objective, explanation, fictional worked example, supplied exercise, reasoned answer and transfer task.

## Completion activity

Fictional: an adult promised to look at Ivo's model before leaving. Departure is now necessary. The model can remain safely on a shelf. Ivo says, “You never have time.” The adult has shouted. There is no other information about Ivo's motives or needs.

Prepare a short response and plan. Identify the adult's broken promise, an observation rather than a diagnosis, a tentative invitation, the departure boundary, two feasible choices, an apology without blame, and a realistic later action. Identify one unknown. Explain why quick agreement is not the only measure of success.

## Reasoned guide

An example: “I said I would look and didn't make time. I'm sorry I shouted. Your model matters. We need to leave now; it can stay on the shelf or go in this suitable box. Would you like me to look with you when we return?” Offer the later time only if realistic. Ivo can decline or remain upset. The reason for Ivo's reaction remains unknown beyond the words supplied; no diagnosis follows.

Check that the box fits and storage is safe. Keep the promise you make next. Measure whether you listened, avoided blame and maintained care, not whether Ivo instantly became cheerful. Other wording can pass if it meets the same criteria.

## Self-check

- Facts and guesses are separated.
- The child can correct the adult or not answer.
- Choices are real and the necessary boundary is clear.
- Repair does not demand affection or forgiveness.
- Care notes respect age and individual needs.
- Serious safety concerns use the appropriate help route.

Revise missing elements. No submission, family story, child photograph or real interaction is required.
