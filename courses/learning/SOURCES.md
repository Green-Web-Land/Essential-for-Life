## Evidence and original design

Goals, respectful feedback prompts and response alternatives are course design choices. They do not assert tested benefits or assign a fixed learning style. An example schedule is illustrative. The final record is deliberately insufficient for a causal conclusion.

All fractions, card rules and source dependencies can be checked from the supplied material without visiting these references. Correct arithmetic is not proof of a learning intervention's effectiveness. No diagnoses, intelligence classifications, guarantees of grades or professional qualifications are made.

## Retrieval limitations

The publisher DOI landing page for the 2008 study returned an error; the full author-hosted paper above was retrieved instead. The individual COR introductory lesson URL also returned an error. Its accessible public homepage supplies the framework used; no claim relies on the unavailable lesson. The IES guide PDF and cited source pages were retrieved.

This is a bounded source review, not a systematic review of all learning research. No learner pilot, independent education review or formal accessibility assessment has been performed. Context and applicability need reconsideration when adapting the audience or activities.
