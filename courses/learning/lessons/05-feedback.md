# 5. Use feedback to revise

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Turn a specific correction into a new attempt.

Adults 18+. Supplied practice records are fictional. Use paper, speech or an accessible equivalent; calculator optional.

## Understand

Useful feedback identifies something in the work and gives you a way to inspect or revise it. “You are bad at this” does neither. You do not have to accept insults as instruction.

For this course, compare a response with the stated rule, identify the exact mismatch, revise it and test a new case. Ask for clarification when feedback conflicts with the reference. A confident comment can also be wrong.

Do not turn one answer into a fixed judgement about a person's ability. A check describes this response under these conditions.

## Worked example

The rule says a card needs an even number AND a stripe for tray K. A learner puts card 8 without a stripe in K. Specific feedback: “You checked evenness; now check the stripe condition too.” Revision: the card does not belong in K.

## Practise

A second response puts card 7 with a stripe in K. Write feedback that names the missing check. Then apply the rule to card 10 with a stripe.

## Suggested reasoning

Useful feedback: “The stripe condition is met, but 7 is odd; both conditions are required.” Card 10 with a stripe meets both and belongs in K. Accept other respectful wording with the same reasoning.

## Try a new situation

Someone says card 10 with a stripe must be excluded but gives no reason. What next? Check: return to the supplied rule, ask which condition fails and do not change a correct answer merely because a comment sounds certain.
