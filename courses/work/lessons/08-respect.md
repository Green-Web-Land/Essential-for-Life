# 8. Respect people and information

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Choose an action that fits permissions and access needs.

Adults 18+. All workplaces, people, policies and task timings in these exercises are fictional. Work on paper; no real workplace access or disclosure is required.

## Understand

Respectful collaboration does not require identical ways of speaking, reading or working. Ask about a useful format rather than assuming that a person's accent, eye contact or disability tells you their competence. Discuss practical access needs through an appropriate channel without asking colleagues to reveal diagnoses.

Information access is also specific. Being able to open a file does not prove permission to redistribute it. Check the purpose, authorized recipient and sharing method. The fictional policies in the examples supply the rules for the exercise; they are not universal privacy law.

Do not put real customer or employee records into an external tool for practice. If AI is used in a workplace, actual authorization and data-handling rules matter. This course needs no AI use and provides no blanket approval to send workplace content to one.

## Worked example

Fictional policy permits sharing an anonymized training schedule internally, but prohibits sharing employee medical notes. Sam asks for a larger-text schedule. Noor can arrange the approved accessible format without including the medical notes or asking why Sam needs it. Access to the folder does not expand the permitted disclosure.

## Practise

A colleague offers their login because your account is not ready. Another asks you to copy a customer spreadsheet into your personal email to work faster. The supplied exercise policy prohibits both actions. Identify a permitted next step and the task impact to report.

## Suggested reasoning

Request access through the designated process and tell the task owner that the needed access is pending. Ask for an approved alternative using non-sensitive training data if appropriate. Do not accept shared credentials or move the spreadsheet to personal email. A deadline does not change the supplied rule.

## Try a new situation

A teammate asks for written instructions instead of a rapid verbal briefing. Draft a practical response that confirms the format and any timing constraint without requiring medical information.
