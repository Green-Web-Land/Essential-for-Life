# Working Well: Skills for a Successful Employee

**A complete introductory course for adults aged 18 and over.**

Learn to clarify tasks, plan realistically, report progress, collaborate, check your work and raise concerns responsibly. Working well includes asking for needed support and respecting limits; it does not mean accepting unsafe work or unlimited demands.

Ten lessons, five diagrams, fictional exercises, suggested reasoning and a final project. No current job, employer participation, workplace records, software installation or AI account is required. Read online or copy this folder for offline learning.

## Before you begin

This is general education, not personalized employment-law advice, job-specific safety training or a guarantee of hiring, promotion or retention. The adult age label is an editorial choice, not tested suitability.

Use only the supplied fictional information. You may skip a scenario or choose a neutral alternative with a facilitator; no explanation or personal disclosure is needed. Do not perform equipment tasks, contact an employer or file a real complaint as an exercise. For actual urgent danger use appropriate local emergency assistance and workplace emergency procedures.

## Start learning

| Lesson | What you will practise |
|---|---|
| [1. Understand your role](lessons/01-role.md) | Separate assigned work, authority and missing information. |
| [2. Clarify a task before committing](lessons/02-clarify.md) | Turn an ambiguous request into a checkable task agreement. |
| [3. Plan realistic work](lessons/03-plan.md) | Build a feasible schedule from durations and dependencies. |
| [4. Communicate useful progress](lessons/04-progress.md) | Write an update that distinguishes facts, uncertainty and decisions. |
| [5. Collaborate and hand over](lessons/05-handoff.md) | Provide enough context for another person to continue. |
| [6. Check quality and handle mistakes](lessons/06-quality.md) | Report a discrepancy and propose a bounded correction. |
| [7. Learn from feedback](lessons/07-feedback.md) | Convert specific feedback into a practical learning step. |
| [8. Respect people and information](lessons/08-respect.md) | Choose an action that fits permissions and access needs. |
| [9. Raise concerns and find support](lessons/09-concerns.md) | Distinguish a routine work question from a safety or rights concern. |
| [10. Final fictional work assignment](lessons/10-project.md) | Produce a feasible plan, honest update and usable handoff. |

## How to study

Read the explanation and worked example, try the practice task, then compare with the suggested reasoning. Complete the fresh situation to see whether you can transfer the idea. Notes, tables, labelled drawings or spoken explanations can all work. No fixed speed or prior course is required.

Durations are fictional teaching assumptions. A plan that depends on missing permissions or impossible timing is not made feasible by a confident promise. Exercise scores describe the submitted reasoning, not a person's worth or suitability for employment.
