## Editorial and legal boundaries

Task checklists, schedules, fictional policies, answer keys and rubric are original teaching designs. They are not validated productivity standards, legal tests or employer policy templates. The sources support limited concepts, not proven effectiveness of this course.

Ontario's general Employment Standards Act guide was found but its full page could not be retrieved during this preparation. No wage rates, entitlement calculations, complaint deadlines or detailed claims from that page are relied on. The verified federal page supplies the coverage/reference distinction; actual rights need current applicable sources or qualified advice.

No course exercise requires reporting a real incident, contacting a service or disclosing workplace information. Service eligibility, response, confidentiality and outcomes are not guaranteed. Avoid importing a federal or Ontario procedure into another setting without checking applicability.
