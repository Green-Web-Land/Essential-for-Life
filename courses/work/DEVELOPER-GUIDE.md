# Developer reuse guide

[Course home](README.md)

Copy the complete folder at a recorded revision, including source notes, licence and images. Required activities have no live repository dependency. Preserve relative paths, alternative text, table headings and adjacent explanations.

Keep fictional labels and timing assumptions with examples. A changed duration or prerequisite can affect a schedule, answer, diagram and rubric. Recompute all affected values; do not display an estimate as a guarantee.

Do not turn the rubric into automated employee evaluation, legal advice, safety clearance or hiring eligibility. Adding workplace uploads, monitoring, saved answers, credentials or personalized advice changes the activity and needs its own privacy/security/suitability assessment. Static files do not collect learner responses.

Maintain accessible reading and an offline route. Test keyboard use, resizing and assistive-technology presentation in your actual renderer; no formal conformance has been established for your implementation.

SVGs are editable code originals with no scripts or external images; PNGs are display versions. Follow [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md), retain attribution and source notices, and identify changes. Recheck dated regional guidance when maintaining an edition. The publisher's internal approval flow adds no restriction to recipients' licence rights.
