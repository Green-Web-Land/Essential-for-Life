# Facilitator guide

[Course home](README.md)

Teach with supplied fiction. Do not ask learners to bring employment contracts, personnel records, passwords, complaints or customer information. This is skills education, not job assessment, legal counselling or machinery training.

## Participation and accessibility

Accept notes, tables, drawings with labels or spoken explanations. Do not grade accent, eye contact, speed or comfort with public speaking. Provide time to read and a paper route. A learner may pass or replace a scenario without explanation. No real employer participation or paired confrontation is required.

For sensitive scenarios in lessons 7 and 9, use a fictional missing-file problem and a supplied building emergency notice to practise choosing different contact types. This alternative demonstrates routing, not knowledge of harassment law. Do not invent local emergency instructions.

If someone raises an actual concern, stop treating it as a class exercise. Do not investigate publicly, promise confidentiality or determine legal rights. Explain your actual role and direct them toward appropriate qualified/local support. Check your setting's applicable responsibilities before facilitating.

## Final project rubric

Each row: 0 = missing or contradicts facts; 1 = partly supported; 2 = clear and consistent. Eight rows give a maximum of 16. Use the result for feedback, not a hiring score or a prediction of job success. Revise unauthorized or unsafe actions regardless of total.

| Criterion | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---|
| Clarification | States the three-section draft, preserved source numbers and no external sending; names two unanswered practical questions. |
| Baseline | Correct sequential schedule and waiting; finish 10:35, effort 80 minutes, elapsed 95, margin 25. |
| Priority conflict | Urgent task gives 11:05 completion, five minutes after original deadline; asks for a decision. |
| Revised plan | Uses Pat's confirmed 11:20 deadline; correction gives 11:15 finish, five-minute margin. |
| Progress | Separates completed, correcting/checking and review-pending states; no unearned acceptance claim. |
| Handoff | Names version, source, correction, checks and next reviewer; file opening is not approval. |
| Permissions | Does not use unauthorized records or invent access; seeks a permitted input or decision. |
| Transfer | Later baseline input gives 10:50 finish, effort 80, elapsed 110, margin ten minutes. |

## Worked scoring examples

“Finish at 10:35 even with the extra 30-minute task” contradicts the sequence: priority-conflict criterion 0. Correcting the finish to 11:05 and identifying the five-minute conflict with a request for a decision earns 2.

“Pat changed the deadline to 11:20; correction and recheck lead to 11:15” earns 2 for the revised plan if the dependency order is retained. The deadline may not be changed by the learner without the explicit scenario update.

“Recipient opened it, so approved” earns 0 for handoff. Naming the actual usability evidence while preserving review-pending status supports full credit.

Ask learners to explain one revision and try the transfer task. Alternative phrasing is welcome. A scheduling error need not be treated as a character judgement. Correct arithmetic is necessary but does not itself establish lawful, safe or authorized real-world work.
