# Curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

Adult beginners 18+. Standalone, paper-based first edition. The aim is to explain a choice using supplied facts, consent and practical constraints—not to judge a learner's family.

| Lesson | Observable learning evidence |
|---|---|
| [1. Families, care and differences](lessons/01-families.md) | Separate stated facts from assumptions about a household. |
| [2. Listen and check understanding](lessons/02-listening.md) | Write a short understanding check that allows correction. |
| [3. Express needs and make requests](lessons/03-requests.md) | Turn a complaint into a specific, negotiable request. |
| [4. Boundaries, consent and privacy](lessons/04-boundaries.md) | Choose a response that respects a stated limit. |

| [8. Support through change](lessons/08-change.md) | Offer bounded help that the recipient can accept, alter or decline. |
| [9. When conversation is not enough](lessons/09-help.md) | Match a fictional concern to an appropriate type of help. |

## Learning sequence

Lessons 1–3 establish facts, listening and requests. Lessons 4–5 apply permission and capacity to shared arrangements. Lessons 6–9 address disagreement, repair, change and the limits of conversation. Lesson 10 combines these skills in a fictional plan.

Every lesson provides an explanation, worked example, exercise, suggested reasoning and a different transfer situation. Learners may revisit or skip material. Before teaching lesson 6 or 9, review the [facilitator guide](FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) so a classroom discussion does not become a disclosure requirement.

## Evidence of learning

Use the final-project rubric to check whether the answer fits the facts, leaves room for refusal, identifies unassigned work and chooses an appropriate support category. Revisions are part of learning. A correct written answer is not proof that a real relationship is safe.

The course has no graded personal questionnaire, psychological screening, diagnosis or certificate of professional competence. A later child or teen edition would need its own content and suitability review.
