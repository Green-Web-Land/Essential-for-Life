## What the sources do not establish

The request scaffold, task agreement, fictional examples, exercises, answer keys and rubric are original editorial teaching designs. They have not been validated as therapy or tested with learners. Source support for a general concept does not establish effectiveness of our particular course.

The material avoids interpreting custody, guardianship, reporting duties, sexual-consent law or individual eligibility. A legal, clinical or urgent safety question requires the relevant local qualified route. The everyday permission examples concern ordinary photographs, objects and communication.

Two Ontario government pages were found in search but full retrieval was unavailable during preparation. No detailed claim about those pages' services is relied on here; the accessible federal directory supplies the regional reference route instead.
