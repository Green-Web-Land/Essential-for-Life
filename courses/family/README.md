# Family Relationships: Communicate and Care

**A complete introductory course for adults aged 18 and over.**

Practise listening, making clear requests, respecting limits and planning shared responsibilities. Learn when an ordinary conversation exercise does not fit the situation.

Ten lessons, five explanatory diagrams, fictional exercises, suggested reasoning and a final project. Read online or copy this folder for offline use. No WebAI, AI account, purchase, family participation or private disclosure is required.

## Before you begin

This is general education, not therapy, diagnosis, family-law advice or an individualized safety plan. It does not promise reconciliation or a particular response from someone else. The adult age label is an editorial audience choice, not tested suitability.

Some lessons mention threats and control without graphic detail. You may pause, skip a scenario or use the neutral alternatives in the facilitator guide. You do not need to explain why. Work with the fiction rather than your own experiences.

If you are in immediate danger in Canada, call 911; elsewhere use your local emergency number. For non-emergency Canadian family-violence support, see [the federal help directory](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/health-promotion/stop-family-violence/services.html). GitHub and the publisher's email are not emergency support services.

## How to use the course

Read the explanation and worked example, try the exercise before opening its suggested reasoning, then attempt the new situation. Answers can be notes, tables, drawings with labels or spoken explanations. The suggested reasoning illustrates a defensible answer; it does not tell you what another person must feel.

No fixed completion speed is required. You can return to one lesson, and no other course is a prerequisite. Completion demonstrates practice with these fictional exercises, not a professional qualification or a tested real-world outcome.
