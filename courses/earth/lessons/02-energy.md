# 2. Energy and change

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Separate an energy flow from the movement of material.

Adults 18+. All named places, scenarios and numbers in exercises are fictional. Work on paper or an accessible equivalent; a calculator is optional.

## Understand

Sunlight supplies energy to Earth's surface and atmosphere. Some incoming radiation is reflected; absorbed energy can warm materials and support processes such as evaporation. Earth also emits infrared radiation to space. NASA's radiation-budget explanation describes these exchanges.

A long-term imbalance between absorbed and emitted energy changes stored energy. This is not a claim that incoming and outgoing energy are exactly equal at every moment. We do not use old global energy-budget numbers as current measurements.

Earth's internal heat is also relevant to its geological activity, as described by USGS. It is distinct from the sunlight driving many surface processes.

Energy and matter are different kinds of quantities. A water arrow follows material; an energy arrow may show radiation or heating. Use matching units before adding anything.

## Worked example

An invented energy account receives 100 energy units, reflects 25 and emits 70 over the same interval. In this simplified account, the net gain is 100 − 25 − 70 = 5 units. These are fictional units, not Earth's measured radiation budget.

## Practise

A second invented account receives 80 units, reflects 20 and emits 65. Calculate its net change. Explain why a negative answer does not mean energy was destroyed.

## Suggested reasoning

80 − 20 − 65 = −5 units: stored energy decreases by 5 in the account. Energy left the chosen system. The calculation assumes the listed transfers are the complete account for the interval, not that every real-world energy transfer is listed.

## Try a new situation

Someone adds 5 litres of water to 5 energy units and reports a total of 10. What is wrong? Check: these are different quantities with incompatible units.
