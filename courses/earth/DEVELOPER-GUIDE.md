# How Earth Works — developer guide

[Course home](README.md)

Copy the entire course folder with lessons, SVG/PNG figures, source notes and licence. Preserve relative links, or update and verify them when relocating the material. Adapt the parent-catalogue link if distributing a standalone copy.

## Preserve meaning

Keep fictional numbers and place names labelled. Retain units, intervals, model boundaries, signs and the distinction between storage and storage change. If a value changes, update every answer, figure, table and guide affected and check the arithmetic again.

Preserve the meanings of arrows. Food-web arrows run from food to consumer; a water arrow follows material; an energy arrow represents a different quantity. Keep text equivalents beside images. Do not turn not-to-scale diagrams into maps or forecasts.

Retain the adult audience and general-education limits. A child-facing adaptation, real field activity or environmental decision aid needs its own suitability review. No course completion score confers professional status.

## Hosting and reuse

These static files do not implement accounts, analytics or environmental sensors. Assess the behaviour of the actual host separately; static files cannot establish that a hosting application collects nothing.

No AI account is required. Any added AI, data collection or real-location advice is outside the reviewed package. Do not use the fictional budgets to generate claims of drinking-water safety, flood probability or emissions offsets.
