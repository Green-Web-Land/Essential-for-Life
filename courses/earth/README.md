# How Earth Works: A Living System

Explore connections among air, water, rocks, ice, climate and ecosystems. **“Living system” is a metaphor for connected processes and life; Earth is not a biological organism.**

For **adult beginners, 18+**. This age label is an editorial choice, not tested suitability. No previous course, equipment, field trip, purchase or AI account is needed.

Read in order or choose a topic through the [curriculum](CURRICULUM.md). Each lesson offers a goal, explanation, fictional worked example, practice, suggested reasoning and a new situation.

All required activities work on paper or an accessible equivalent. A calculator is optional. Supplied datasets and places are fictional. You do not need to visit, sample or change any natural area, collect private information or run an experiment.

![Air, water, solid Earth, life and ice interact. The boxes are study categories, not sealed compartments.](images/systems.png)

Text equivalent: Earth's air, water, solid material, living things and frozen water interact. A model selects connections for a purpose; it does not show every process.

## Learn to read a model

The ten lessons and five diagrams help you trace pathways, interpret simple accounts and identify missing information. The final watershed activity compares two invented budgets. Balanced arithmetic does not make the model a valid description of a real place.

This is general education, not environmental consulting, a drinking-water assessment, a disaster warning, a local climate forecast or a professional qualification. Diagrams are simplified and not to scale. No learner pilot, independent scientific validation or formal accessibility assessment has been performed.
