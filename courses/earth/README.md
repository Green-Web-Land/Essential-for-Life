# How Earth Works: A Living System

[Essential Knowledge for Life](../../README.md)

**Course introduction · full course planned.**

Explore connections among air, water, rocks, climate and ecosystems. “Living system” describes these connections; it does not mean Earth is a biological organism.

## What this course is intended to cover

- Explore connections among air, water, rocks and living ecosystems.
- Use diagrams to follow a process and explain its relationships.
- Consider evidence about change and distinguish a model from the world it describes.

## Example of a planned activity

Draw a connection among rain, soil, plants and a nearby waterway, then explain the parts.

This describes a future learning activity, not a complete exercise or a tested learning outcome. The full edition will supply the explanation, fictional material, practice steps and suggested reasoning.

## Readers and practical needs

The title's living-system framing refers to connected processes and ecosystems, not a claim that Earth is a biological organism.
