# How Earth Works — glossary

[Course home](README.md)

Plain-language meanings for this course; definitions in specialist work may be more precise.

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Atmosphere | The gases surrounding Earth. |
| Hydrosphere | Earth's water, considered as a system. |
| Cryosphere | Frozen water, including snow and ice. |
| Geosphere | The solid Earth in this course's system model. |
| Biosphere | Living organisms considered in their Earth-system relationships. |
| Energy flow | Transfer of energy, distinct from an amount of material. |
| Reservoir/store | Material held within a specified part of a system. |
| Transfer | Movement between stores over a defined interval. |
| Evaporation | Liquid water changing to vapour. |
| Condensation | Water vapour changing to liquid. |
| Transpiration | Release of water vapour by plants. |
| Infiltration | Water entering soil from the surface. |
| Groundwater | Subsurface water in saturated pores or cracks. |
| Weathering | Breakdown or chemical alteration of rock in place. |
| Erosion | Removal and transport of surface material. |
| Deposition | Accumulation of transported material. |
| Food web | A model of feeding relationships. |
| Weather | Atmospheric conditions over shorter periods. |
| Climate | Longer-term patterns and variation of conditions. |
| Watershed | Land draining toward a shared outlet; real subsurface relationships can add complexity. |
| Budget | An account of inputs, outputs and change in storage for a boundary and interval. |
| Model | A selective representation used to explore a question. |
