# How Earth Works — curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

Adult beginners 18+. Use supplied fiction on paper; no time limit, account or fieldwork. A calculator or assistance reading the material is optional.

| Lesson | Goal |
|---|---|
| [1. Earth as connected systems](lessons/01-systems.md) | Trace a connection without treating a diagram as the whole world. |
| [2. Energy and change](lessons/02-energy.md) | Separate an energy flow from the movement of material. |
| [3. Water on the move](lessons/03-water.md) | Follow more than one route through a water model. |
| [4. Rocks and a changing surface](lessons/04-surface.md) | Distinguish processes and timescales that shape land. |
| [5. Soil and life](lessons/05-soil.md) | Describe soil as a varied mixture with several functions. |
| [6. Ecosystems and relationships](lessons/06-ecosystems.md) | Read a food web without predicting every population change. |
| [7. Weather and climate](lessons/07-climate.md) | Choose evidence appropriate to the timescale of a claim. |
| [8. Carbon and human influence](lessons/08-carbon.md) | Separate a carbon store from a transfer and a global claim from a local forecast. |
| [9. Reading evidence and models](lessons/09-models.md) | Separate a model output, an observation and missing information. |
| [10. A watershed on paper](lessons/10-watershed.md) | Explain connected processes using a bounded fictional account. |

## Route and outcomes

Lessons 1–3 connect systems, energy and water. Lessons 4–6 examine surface material, soil and ecosystems. Lessons 7–8 distinguish climate evidence and carbon transfers. Lessons 9–10 combine model reading and a fictional water budget.

Intended outcomes: trace a labelled connection, distinguish a store from a transfer, calculate a simple account, explain a model limitation and ask what evidence is missing. These are learning goals, not validated outcomes or professional competence.

Every activity includes suggested reasoning and a transfer check. Learners may skip an example or answer orally. No location history, health information, water sample or physical demonstration is required.
