## Original material and checks

All numerical examples and named places are newly authored fiction. Budget identities and arithmetic are checked, but that does not validate a physical model. No rainfall partition, soil ratio, energy flux or carbon amount in an exercise is a measured Earth value.

Diagrams are original code-authored learning aids with AI assistance. They do not copy source illustrations, which may contain third-party material even on government sites.

Two historical USGS tectonics pages and a NOAA food-web page could not be fully retrieved. The included geology and ecology explanations instead use the retrieved NPS and OpenStax references above. No essential included claim relies on those unavailable pages.

## Review and updating

Recheck affected references before changing scientific claims. Date changing climate or observational claims, distinguish observation from model output and retain unknowns. Current local hazards and environmental conditions require appropriate local evidence and expertise.

The course has not had a learner pilot, independent scientific validation, professional legal certification or formal accessibility assessment.
