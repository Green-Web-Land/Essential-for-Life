# How Earth Works — illustration provenance

[Course home](README.md)

Five original code-authored diagrams were created with AI assistance. Each is provided as an SVG and PNG. No source photographs, logos, maps or third-party illustrations are embedded.

| Files in images/ | Meaning |
|---|---|
| systems.svg / systems.png | Study categories and selected relationships among Earth systems. |
| water.svg / water.png | Selected water pathways; not all exchanges or travel times. |
| surface.svg / surface.png | Weathering, transport and deposition, not a complete rock cycle. |
| food-web.svg / food-web.png | Invented feeding links with arrows from food to consumer. |
| watershed.svg / watershed.png | Two fictional interval water accounts; no real-location estimate. |

Text equivalents accompany lesson images. Figures are simplified and not to scale. In the budget figure, storage increase is not another flow leaving the system. It is the remainder of input after outputs in the defined interval.
