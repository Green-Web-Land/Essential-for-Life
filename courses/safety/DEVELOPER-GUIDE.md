# Developer reuse guide

This package is static Markdown with original SVG and PNG diagrams. Copy a pinned, approved course revision with its licence, source map and asset notices. Do not depend on a changing draft branch. Preserve relative paths beneath courses/safety/.

## Content contract

- Ten lesson files, eight supporting Markdown files and five SVG/PNG pairs.
- Preserve audience, fictional labels, emergency-help priority, geographic limits and alternatives beside the material they qualify.
- Render images with their alt text and adjacent text equivalent. Do not remove text equivalents in a compact view.
- Keep lesson navigation and source links usable without sign-in. Do not replace source-based help routes with generated answers.
- Do not turn the six-task rubric into a risk predictor, triage engine or certification.
- Do not turn paper practice into an auto-dial, message form, exposure simulator or real-world test.
- Do not infer that a static page collects data. If you add analytics, forms, accounts or answer storage, assess those features and disclose actual practices separately.

## Checks after integration

Verify relative links, image sizing, keyboard and assistive-technology behaviour in the actual host. This package has no product-runtime or formal accessibility certification. Check numbers and service scope against current official sources when updating or localising; do not assume short codes work everywhere or that ordinary text messaging reaches emergency dispatch. Have adaptations affecting guidance reviewed before deployment.

The diagrams are learning aids, not building plans or official warning signs. Preserve their captions. Translations need meaning and local-service review; literal translation alone does not validate emergency guidance.

Retain attribution and describe changes under [the licence](LICENSE.md). Third-party linked sites are not included in this package or covered by its licence.
