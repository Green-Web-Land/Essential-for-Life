# Sources and help-route notes

Checked 2026-09-11. Primary official sources support the bounded guidance indicated below. This course is original AI-assisted educational writing with invented exercises; the sources did not endorse, test or certify it.

## Telephone examples

| Service | Number | Purpose and geographic limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency response | 911 | Police, fire or medical emergency in Toronto/Ontario; not a universal worldwide access claim | S1, S5, S10 |
| 211 Ontario | 211 | Non-emergency community and social-service referral in Ontario; phone service listed 24 hours a day | S2 |
| Toronto city services | 311 | Toronto municipal service information; not emergency response | S1 |
| Health811 | 811 | Ontario non-urgent registered-nurse advice and health-service navigation; listed day and night | S3 |
| Ontario Poison Centre | 1-800-268-9017 | Ontario poison exposure concerns/questions; telephone advice listed 24 hours a day | S4 |

Poison advice may be time-sensitive. Unconsciousness or difficulty breathing calls for emergency response, not waiting in another service's queue (S5). The course does not supply a complete symptom list or a method of ruling out an emergency.

Numbers are examples for the stated locations, not a guarantee of connection from every phone or country. Accessibility, language, text/chat channels, eligibility and privacy practices differ by provider. No universal ordinary-SMS access or blanket confidentiality promise is made. During preparedness, consult the provider's official access information for your needs; do not place a practice emergency call. In an actual emergency, do not delay help to research the course.

A referral does not guarantee service availability or a response time. Readers elsewhere must use their own official local services. No calls or live service tests were made for this course.

## Source map

### S1 — Toronto: who to call

[Official source](https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/public-safety-alerts/who-to-call-in-an-emergency/). Emergency police/fire/medical route, location and dispatcher questions; Toronto municipal service route. Its legacy Telehealth wording is not used.

### S2 — 211 Ontario: FAQ

[Official source](https://211ontario.ca/about/faq/). Ontario non-emergency community referrals and telephone availability. No blanket confidentiality guarantee.

### S3 — Ontario Health atHome: guide, printed page 7

[Official source](https://www.ontariohealthathome.ca/blobohahprod4cd80afe1b/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OHaH-Welcome-Booklet-EN.pdf). Health811 number, Ontario scope and non-urgent nurse advice; accessible official replacement for unavailable Ontario landing pages.

### S4 — Ontario Poison Centre: Get Help Now

[Official source](https://www.ontariopoisoncentre.ca/get-help/get-help-now-landing/get-help-now/). Current Ontario poison telephone advice and availability.

### S5 — Ontario Poison Centre: first-aid sheet

[Official source](https://www.ontariopoisoncentre.ca/siteassets/pdfs/english/50738-opc_firstaideng.pdf). Only emergency escalation for unconsciousness or breathing difficulty is used. Older sheet; not reproduced as treatment training.

### S6 — Toronto: Home Fire Escape Planning

[Official source](https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/public-safety-alerts/safety-tips-prevention/home-high-rise-school-workplace-safety/home-fire-escape-planning/). Once outside, do not re-enter; call emergency help. No universal indoor escape procedure is derived.

### S7 — Health Canada: household chemical safety

[Official source](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/home-safety/household-chemical-safety.html). Do not mix household chemicals; follow labels; prompt poison advice for suspected harm.

### S8 — Electrical Safety Authority: outdoor safety

[Official source](https://esasafe.com/safety/home-electrical-safety/outdoor-safety/). Damaged extension cords can cause shock or fire; recognising a defect is not repair training.

### S9 — Environment and Climate Change Canada: Weather Alerts

[Official source](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/environment/weather/severeweather/weather-alerts.html). Read full alert text including location, timing, impacts and actions. Fictional park notice is original, not an actual government alert.

### S10 — Public Health Agency of Canada: family violence services

[Official source](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/health-promotion/stop-family-violence/services.html). Local support directory and immediate-danger emergency route.

### S11 — Public Health Agency of Canada: planning for safety

[Official source](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/health-promotion/stop-family-violence/plan-your-safety.html). Individual support planning and risk of monitored devices. No guarantee that deleting history removes traces; obsolete browser instructions not taught.

### S12 — Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: protect yourself

[Official source](https://centreantifraude-antifraudcentre.ca/protect-protegez-eng.htm?wbdisable=true). Pressure, spoofed identities and independently obtained contact routes; no generic password length or other unrelated advice adopted.

## Evidence limits

The unavailable Ontario Health811 programme/Your Health landing pages were replaced with Ontario Health atHome's current official guide, printed page 7, which explicitly states the number and non-urgent purpose. Toronto's legacy Telehealth naming is not adopted. The older poison sheet is used only for emergency escalation, alongside the current Poison Centre contact page. The older browser-menu instructions on S11 are not adopted.

Weather advice is limited to reading full current alerts and following their directions; no storm categories or route-selection algorithm is taught. Fire scenarios place the learner already outside to avoid implying one indoor evacuation procedure fits every building. No treatment, chemical handling or rescue method is taught.

Sources may change after this check. A source-backed statement is not proof of learner effectiveness, legal certification or a guarantee against harm. Third-party pages retain their own rights. No third-party images or lesson text are copied.
