# Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this course |
|---|---|
| Hazard | Something with the potential to cause harm. |
| Observation | A specific detail noticed or supplied in a case. |
| Assumption | Something accepted without enough supporting information. |
| Uncertainty | A relevant fact that remains unknown; not permission to ignore danger. |
| Emergency | A situation needing urgent emergency response; this glossary is not a diagnostic test. |
| Routine enquiry | A non-emergency request for information or service. |
| Poison exposure | Contact with a potentially harmful substance, for which poison advice may be needed. |
| Referral | Guidance towards another service; not guaranteed access to it. |
| Jurisdiction | The place or service area where a route applies. |
| Dispatcher | A person handling an emergency call and coordinating the appropriate response. |
| Coercion | Pressure or threats used to control someone's choices. |
| Spoofing | Imitating an identity, such as a displayed sender or number. |
| Independent contact route | A route established separately from the suspicious request. |
| Safeguarding | Action to protect people from abuse or harm; individual circumstances matter. |
| Qualified help | Help from a person or service with the relevant role and expertise, rather than confidence alone. |
