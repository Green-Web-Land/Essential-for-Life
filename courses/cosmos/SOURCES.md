## Facts, fiction and approximations

Real names and basic scientific concepts are source-backed. Exercise line positions, light values, distances and periods are deliberately invented. They are not telescope data or physical predictions. Original worked examples and critical-reading prompts were written for this course.

The age estimate is explicitly approximate and dated. The light-year conversion is rounded. A fixed-distance light-delay example is not a cosmological distance formula. No number of confirmed exoplanets, inhabited worlds, moons or current missions is claimed.

## Retrieval and limits

The listed resources were available through browser retrieval. The older combined dark-matter/dark-energy URL redirected to the dark-matter page; dark energy was checked separately. Search results were used to locate the MAST archive and Hubble glossary, then their pages were retrieved.

Source pages sometimes contain old mission wording, changing counts, simplifying analogies or third-party illustrations. Only the mapped concepts were used; retrieval is not endorsement of every line on a page. No unresolved essential retrieval gap remains for the claims included.

No independent scientific review, learner pilot or formal accessibility assessment has been performed. Verify source updates before adapting changing claims. An added observing activity, new dataset or child-facing edition needs its own review.
