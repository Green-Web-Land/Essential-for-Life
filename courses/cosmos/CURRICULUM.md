# Exploring the Cosmos — curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

## Audience and purpose

Adult beginners 18+. Learn to describe systems and scale, reason with supplied information and keep conclusions within the evidence. No prerequisite course or astronomy equipment. The age designation and objectives are editorial, not results of learner testing.

## Learning route

| Lesson | Objective | Practice evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [1. Our address in space](lessons/01-address.md) | Distinguish membership in a system from distance on a map. | Correct a membership caption. |
| [2. Size, distance and time](lessons/02-scale.md) | Use a stated scale and distinguish length from elapsed time. | Compute drawing positions and a gap. |
| [3. Motion and gravity](lessons/03-motion.md) | Separate rotation from orbit and recognize a simplified model. | Compare fictional rotation and orbit periods. |
| [4. Meet the solar system](lessons/04-solar-system.md) | Classify familiar objects using more than their appearance. | Match named objects and explain missing classification information. |
| [5. The Sun and other stars](lessons/05-stars.md) | Explain why stars do not all follow one identical life story. | Reject a universal stellar life story. |
| [6. Learning from light](lessons/06-light.md) | Distinguish a recorded signal from an interpretation. | Distinguish full and partial toy line-pattern matches. |
| [7. Galaxies and cosmic history](lessons/07-history.md) | Separate evidence about the universe from a complete explanation of everything. | Separate travel delay from age. |
| [8. Worlds beyond our solar system](lessons/08-other-worlds.md) | Explain what an exoplanet clue does and does not establish. | Compare relative and absolute signal drops. |
| [9. Open questions and space claims](lessons/09-claims.md) | Keep curiosity while checking the strength of a claim. | Check an unsupported discovery claim. |
| [10. Build a space explanation](lessons/10-project.md) | Combine a model, numerical checks and a carefully limited conclusion. | Write a bounded explanation from two separate exhibits. |

## How to study

Read the explanation, attempt the practice before opening its reasoning, then explain the transfer example in your own words. Answers may be spoken, written or represented with a table. Use a calculator if arithmetic would otherwise obstruct the main idea.

The final six-task project brings earlier skills together. The facilitator guide supplies a 12-point learning rubric and worked keys, not a credential or pass certificate. Missing information is often part of the correct answer.

All activities use supplied fictional records or simple classification questions. There is no telescope use, Sun observation, field trip, account setup or personal-data collection.
