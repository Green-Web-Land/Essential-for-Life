# 9. Open questions and space claims

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Keep curiosity while checking the strength of a claim.

Adults 18+. All invented records and model numbers are labelled. Work on paper or an accessible equivalent; calculator optional.

## Understand

Some questions remain open even when parts of an explanation are well supported. NASA describes evidence for dark matter's gravitational effects while its physical nature remains unresolved. Dark energy names the unknown cause associated with accelerated cosmic expansion; it is not another name for dark matter.

NASA's search-for-life material, checked on 11 September 2026, treats life beyond Earth as a research question rather than an established discovery. Neither an unexplained signal nor a compelling illustration is sufficient proof.

Use four labels: observation (what was recorded), inference (an interpretation supported by evidence and assumptions), hypothesis (an explanation to test), and speculation (an idea without adequate support here). A statement may need more than one label as evidence develops.

## Worked example

Fictional report: “The instrument recorded a repeating dip. A transiting body is one possible explanation. The image shows an imagined ocean world.” The first sentence reports a supplied observation, the second proposes an interpretation, and the image is an illustration, not a measured surface.

## Practise

A fictional post says “Scientists cannot explain this flash, so it must be an alien spacecraft.” Name the reasoning gap. Write two questions that would help check it without contacting anyone or downloading files.

## Suggested reasoning

Not knowing one explanation does not establish another. Ask where the original measurement and uncertainty are documented, and which alternatives or instrument checks were considered. A useful response can be “The supplied evidence is insufficient to decide.”

## Try a new situation

A later paper corrects a measurement. Should every related conclusion automatically be discarded? Check: assess which claims depend on that measurement and how the correction changes them; avoid both automatic dismissal and automatic acceptance.
