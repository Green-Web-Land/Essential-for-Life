# 1. Our address in space

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Distinguish membership in a system from distance on a map.

Adults 18+. All invented records and model numbers are labelled. Work on paper or an accessible equivalent; calculator optional.

## Understand

Earth belongs to the solar system, whose star is the Sun. The solar system lies within our galaxy, the Milky Way. Galaxies are part of the wider universe. These are different levels of description, not four neighbouring destinations.

A galaxy contains many stars and other material held together by gravity. Our Sun is one star among many. A solar system and a galaxy are not interchangeable names.

A diagram can show membership without showing distances, sizes or a physical boundary. In particular, an outer box labelled “universe” does not establish a wall or a measured edge.

![Earth belongs to the solar system, within the Milky Way, within the universe. Arrows indicate membership, not distance.](../images/address.png)

Text equivalent: Earth belongs to the solar system, within the Milky Way, within the universe. Arrows indicate membership, not distance.

## Worked example

A fictional museum card reads “Earth → solar system → Milky Way → universe.” Its arrows mean “belongs within.” Measuring an arrow on the card would not give a travel distance.

## Practise

Explain what is wrong with these captions:
1. “The Milky Way is one of the planets.”
2. “Earth is outside the Milky Way because it is inside the solar system.”
3. “The boxes prove the distance between Earth and the Sun.”

Rewrite one caption accurately.

## Suggested reasoning

The Milky Way is a galaxy. Being in the solar system is compatible with being in the Milky Way. The boxes do not carry a distance scale. A useful rewrite is “Earth is a planet in the solar system, within the Milky Way.”

## Try a new situation

A new diagram uses arrows for the direction of travel instead of membership. What must change? Check: its legend must say so; do not carry one diagram's arrow meaning into another.
