# 10. Final project: research and share responsibly

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Learning goal:** Produce a supported answer, an uncertainty note and an unsent message.

Use only the fictional cards below. They describe an invented Saturday repair workshop at an invented library. No live search, booking, account, download or message is required. Dates are part of the scenario, not a real event announcement.

**Card A — organiser notice, 1 June.** Workshop on 8 June, starts 10:00, public information at the organiser's established page.

**Card B — organiser correction, 3 June.** Same workshop now starts 11:00. This replaces the time in the 1 June notice. The workshop remains on 8 June.

**Card C — community repost, undated.** Repeats Card A's 10:00 time. Does not cite a later check.

**Card D — advertisement.** “Guaranteed entry! Pay now and upload ID.” No evidence of connection to the organiser.

**Card E — organiser facilities note, 2 June.** Entrance has a ramp. No information about accessible toilets, room layout or individual assistance.

Your friend asks: “When does it start, and can everyone in our group attend comfortably?” Your task is to answer only what these cards support. An honest unresolved question is part of a successful result.

## Worked example

A model opening is: “The latest organiser correction says 11:00 on 8 June (Card B, 3 June), replacing the earlier 10:00 notice.” It gives both the answer and the reason for preferring it.

## Try it — paper is enough

Submit on paper: (1) a time answer with source; (2) two sentences distinguishing known access information from unknowns; (3) an unsent question to the organiser; (4) a decision about D; (5) a viewing-only sharing plan for your summary. Do not include real contact details.

## Answer and reasoning

Use B for time; C repeats superseded information. E supports a ramp only, not universal comfort or accessibility. Ask about relevant facilities without naming anyone's health information. Do not pay or upload ID based on D. Propose a verified recipient and limited viewing access, or a paper copy. Another cautious answer can pass if it explains its evidence.

## Use it somewhere else

Apply the same method to another fictional topic: identify the question, compare sources, state uncertainty and minimise disclosure. Explain one change you would make if the decision had serious personal consequences.

## Sources and limits

Completion means you have practised these skills; it is not professional certification. Use the [facilitator rubric](../FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) to review reasoning and revise your work.
