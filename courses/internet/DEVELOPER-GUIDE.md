# Developer reuse guide

[Course home](README.md)

This folder is a standalone static course. Copy the whole folder, including lessons, PNG/SVG images, sources and licence, at the publication revision you intend to use. It does not need a runtime service or a live dependency on this repository.

## Preserve a usable course

- Keep relative paths or update every reference when moving files.
- Render headings, tables, links, captions and image alternative text.
- Keep answer reasoning available for self-study.
- Retain the paper route and avoid requiring sign-in to complete an exercise.
- Keep fictional examples labelled; do not turn example addresses into operational endpoints.
- Retain licence, attribution, source and asset-provenance information.

PNG files are convenient for display; SVG files are editable originals. They use local text and shapes, with no external resources or scripts. Text explanations accompany the images. The diagrams simplify concepts and are not screenshots of tested interfaces.

## Check an integration

Open every lesson and its previous/next/home links. Check image readability at the actual display size and test keyboard navigation and screen-reader presentation in your own interface. Those integration checks have not been performed by this course's author. Do not claim accessibility conformance from the presence of alt text alone.

The static files do not collect answers. If you add analytics, accounts, uploads, recording or assessment storage, define and review those new data flows and applicable requirements separately. They are not covered by this static course's review.

## Adaptation

[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md) permits commercial adaptation with its conditions. Identify the source revision and your changes, retain required notices, and avoid implying that the original publisher approved your edition. Review factual links and documentation as interfaces change. Child-focused or high-stakes adaptations need appropriate review for that changed scope.
