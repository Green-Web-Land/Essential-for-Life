# Internet curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

**Audience:** adult beginners, 18+. **Prerequisites:** none beyond being able to follow the text, independently or with a reader. Computers background can help but is not required. **Materials:** course pages and paper, or an accessible way to record answers. No account or live service is necessary.

**Purpose:** use online information and communication with an understanding of evidence, permissions and limits.

| Lesson | Outcome | Practice evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. The Internet and the web](lessons/01-internet-web.md) | Distinguish a network, the Internet, a website and an application. | Classify software, service and local copy. |
| [2. Connections and requests](lessons/02-connections.md) | Explain a request and choose a small, reversible check when access fails. | Separate an observation from a diagnosis. |
| [3. Browsers, addresses and links](lessons/03-addresses.md) | Read an example address without mistaking a familiar word for its owner. | Mark a host and explain a deceptive label. |
| [4. Search with a clear question](lessons/04-search.md) | Refine a search and treat results as leads to evaluate. | Refine a question without private details. |
| [5. Trace and check sources](lessons/05-sources.md) | Separate a claim, its evidence and the uncertainty that remains. | Write a dated source note. |
| [6. Email and messages](lessons/06-messages.md) | Prepare a useful message while limiting unnecessary disclosure. | Prepare a minimal unsent message. |
| [7. Accounts and permissions](lessons/07-accounts.md) | Distinguish signing in from granting access, and choose limited permissions. | Choose limited permissions on paper. |
| [8. Downloads and cloud services](lessons/08-files-cloud.md) | Distinguish downloading, synchronising and backing up. | Classify transfers, sync and backup. |
| [9. Privacy and deceptive requests](lessons/09-privacy-scams.md) | Recognise an unsafe request and explain what private browsing does not do. | Correct an anonymity claim. |
| [10. Final project: research and share responsibly](lessons/10-project.md) | Produce a supported answer, an uncertainty note and an unsent message. | Produce an evidence-based sharing plan. |

## Pacing and assessment

Plan roughly 20–35 minutes for each of lessons 1–9 and 30–50 minutes for the final project; adjust for reading, breaks and discussion. These are estimates. Speed is not assessed.

Read the explanation, work the exercise before checking its answer, compare reasoning and revise. There can be more than one defensible answer. The [facilitator guide](FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) provides an observable final-project rubric. Revisit the relevant lesson when a criterion is missed.

Optional browsing in lesson 4 adds experience but is not a pass requirement. A facilitator can read all text and describe each diagram. A learner may answer orally without a recording. These alternatives support participation; they do not establish formal accessibility compliance.

## Boundaries

This is a complete introductory course, not network administration training or individual legal, financial or security incident advice. It does not require a real incident. Future extensions can explore advanced topics separately; they are not prerequisites for this edition.
