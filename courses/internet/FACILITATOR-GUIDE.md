# Facilitator guide

[Course home](README.md) · [Curriculum](CURRICULUM.md)

## Prepare a session

Use the ten lessons in order, or revisit a skill after checking the learner's understanding. Print the text and source cards when offline. Read text aloud if useful; allow spoken answers without recording. Do not ask learners to reveal browsing histories, credentials, financial details or a personal scam experience.

Explain that mistakes in fictional exercises are a chance to revise. Ask “What evidence supports that?” rather than rewarding confidence or speed. Make the answer sections available for self-study; they are not secret exam material.

## Teaching prompts

1. Ask which tasks can work from a saved local copy.
2. Ask what an error observation does **not** prove.
3. Compare the printed host with the link label, without visiting either.
4. Turn a broad phrase into a focused question.
5. Ask whether two sources are actually copies of one notice.
6. Remove unnecessary recipients and attachments from a draft.
7. Match read-only needs to viewing access.
8. Ask how a deletion could reach another location.
9. Separate local browsing privacy from online identity.
10. Review conclusions against the actual source cards.

Never have learners test suspicious links, send messages, install tools or change real account settings for credit. Optional lesson 4 browsing uses a harmless query on a permitted device; the paper alternative is equally valid.

## Final-project rubric

Use [Lesson 10](lessons/10-project.md). Mark each criterion Demonstrated or Revise, and give the reason.

| Criterion | Demonstrated when the learner… |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Chooses 11:00 on 8 June and attributes the correction to Card B. |
| Conflicts | Explains why A/C do not establish the current time. |
| Uncertainty | Separates a ramp from unverified broader access needs. |
| Minimal disclosure | Drafts a relevant question without personal or group records. |
| Deceptive request | Declines D's unsupported payment/ID request and proposes verification. |
| Sharing | Chooses a limited recipient/viewing plan or paper copy and notes copies can spread. |

The instructional completion target is all six demonstrated, with revision allowed and no time limit. This is an author-designed rubric, not a validated test or certification. Do not issue a professional qualification claim.

## Feedback and adaptation

A useful comment identifies the missing evidence and points to a lesson. For example: “Card E mentions a ramp only. Revisit lesson 5 and name what remains unknown.”

Review service-specific details before demonstrating a real interface. This edition has documentary checks only, with no learner pilot. Adaptations for children need their own audience and safeguarding review; simply reducing vocabulary is insufficient. Retain [reuse notices](LICENSE.md), identify changes, and avoid implying endorsement.
