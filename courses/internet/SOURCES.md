## What was and was not verified

Source inspection supports the stated conceptual claims. Exercises, source cards, troubleshooting reasoning and the rubric are original teaching designs, not measured learning outcomes. No browser/device workflow, cloud recovery, email sending or learner trial was executed. Interface wording, permissions and recovery options must be checked in the actual service before use.

No claim is made that this source list is a complete security inventory. A static general course cannot settle an individual incident or legal obligation. The fictional final project deliberately permits uncertainty.
