# Computers: What They Are and How They Help

[Essential Knowledge for Life](../../README.md)

**Course introduction · full course planned.**

Understand hardware and software; organise files; use applications; learn about accounts, backups and troubleshooting.

## What this course is intended to cover

- Recognise the parts of a computer and the role of an operating system.
- Create, name, organise and locate files using practice material.
- Explore accounts, accessible settings, backups and a careful troubleshooting process.

## Example of a planned activity

Save and organise a practice document, then explain how you would keep a separate backup.

This describes a future learning activity, not a complete exercise or a tested learning outcome. The full edition will supply the explanation, fictional material, practice steps and suggested reasoning.

## Readers and practical needs

No previous computer experience will be assumed. Practical lessons will identify the device and software used, with a paper-based alternative where possible.

The intended age range and guidance for supporters will be stated for the complete edition. This overview is not an age-suitability assessment. No WebAI installation or AI account will be required; AI use will be optional.
