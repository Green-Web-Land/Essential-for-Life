# Developer and adaptation guide

[Course home](README.md)

This folder is a standalone Markdown course. Copy the whole folder at an explicitly selected revision, including LICENSE.md, SOURCES.md, ASSET-SOURCES.md and images. Do not require a live branch or another course to supply essential teaching material.

Render relative lesson links, tables and image alternative text. PNGs are the reading assets; matching SVGs are editable diagram sources. Captions and text equivalents must remain available, including when images fail to load. Test your own renderer, keyboard navigation, zoom, text wrapping and links: the author checks on this package do not test an integrated application.

Keep version-sensitive Windows examples labelled. If adapting them to another platform, verify the new steps and disclose the adaptation. Preserve paper alternatives and fictional data. Adding analytics, uploads, accounts or paid services changes the delivery model and needs its own review and accurate disclosures.

Retain attribution and licence information and describe modifications. Third-party websites are references, not bundled material licensed by this course. Publication and integration are separate actions; copying content does not establish application or learner validation.
