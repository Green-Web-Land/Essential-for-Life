## Editorial material

The planning method, fictional people, labels, exercises, answers and diagrams are newly authored with AI assistance. They illustrate reasoning and are not clinical case studies, validated interventions or evidence of anyone's actual circumstances.

Lessons 1, 9 and 10 combine planning and scope distinctions; they do not supply a symptom triage protocol, provider directory or guarantee of services. Professional qualifications, eligibility and availability depend on the actual location and question.

## Updating

Recheck affected references before changing factual guidance. If a source conflicts with individual professional instructions, do not use the lesson to override those instructions. Ask the relevant professional. When a source cannot support an essential claim, remove or resolve the claim before distributing an update.

The course has not had clinical validation or an independent professional legal review. Its internal readiness review is not represented as either.
