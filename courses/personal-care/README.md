# Everyday Personal Care

[Essential Knowledge for Life](../../README.md)

**Course introduction · full course planned.**

Explore everyday hygiene, grooming, clothing care, rest and manageable personal routines, with attention to access needs and when to seek qualified guidance.

## What this course is intended to cover

- Explore supplies, routine planning and practical access needs.
- Explore body and hand hygiene, oral care, hair and nail care, and clean clothing.
- Consider rest, comfort and manageable routines, with suitable support when needed.
- Use appropriate sources for health-related guidance; bathing and oral hygiene are parts of this wider scope.
- Distinguish general guidance from situations that need individual professional advice.

## Example of a planned activity

Compare two fictional routines and identify what information or qualified guidance is needed to adapt them.

This describes a future learning activity, not a complete exercise or a tested learning outcome. The full edition will supply the explanation, fictional material, practice steps and suggested reasoning.

## Readers and practical needs

The course will account for age, ability and access to water or supplies. It will not prescribe one universal routine, diagnose conditions or connect personal worth to appearance.

The intended age range and guidance for supporters will be stated for the complete edition. This overview is not an age-suitability assessment. No WebAI installation or AI account will be required; AI use will be optional.
