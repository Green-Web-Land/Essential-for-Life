# 5. Everyday oral care

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Explain basic adult guidance and identify an individual-care question.

Adults 18+. All people, labels and scenarios used in exercises are fictional. Work on paper; no physical demonstration or personal disclosure is required. You may skip a scenario.

## Understand

The NHS adult guidance recommends brushing with fluoride toothpaste twice daily for about two minutes each time, including before bed, covering the inner, outer and chewing surfaces. It advises spitting out excess toothpaste without rinsing immediately afterwards. A dental professional can advise on cleaning between teeth and on individual needs.

These are general adult principles from a UK public-health source, not a personalized prescription or a requirement to buy a particular brand. Follow local product directions and individual dental advice. This course does not choose fluoride concentration, treat pain or prescribe a routine for dentures, implants or other special circumstances.

A schedule on paper is useful only if it includes the necessary supplies and access. Needing an adapted grip or assistance is a practical question, not a failure.

## Worked example

Fictional Noor plans two brushing occasions but can only grip one of the available brush handles comfortably. Noor asks a dental professional about a suitable option rather than assuming the most expensive brush is required. The exercise does not assess brushing technique.

## Practise

A fictional plan says “brush once, rinse immediately, and ignore difficulty reaching the back teeth.” Identify the differences from the general guidance above and one question for a dental professional.

## Suggested reasoning

The general reference recommends two occasions and not rinsing immediately. Difficulty reaching tooth surfaces needs appropriate technique or adaptation advice, not dismissal. Ask how to clean those areas with the person's actual access needs. Do not diagnose the cause.

## Try a new situation

Write a question about between-teeth cleaning for a fictional adult with no established method. Do not select a tool size from this exercise. [NHS oral-care guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-teeth-and-gums/how-to-keep-your-teeth-clean/).
