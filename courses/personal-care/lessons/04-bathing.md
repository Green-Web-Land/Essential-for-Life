# 4. Body hygiene and bathing

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Plan preparation and recognise when a routine needs individual guidance.

Adults 18+. All people, labels and scenarios used in exercises are fictional. Work on paper; no physical demonstration or personal disclosure is required. You may skip a scenario.

## Understand

Prepare supplies, a clean towel, privacy and a way to get assistance if needed before beginning a real routine. The planning exercise does not teach transfers, balancing in a bath or care of wounds or medical devices. A need for mobility assistance should be addressed with appropriate qualified guidance, not an improvised technique.

Skin needs differ. The American Academy of Dermatology's guidance specifically for dry skin favours warm water, brief bathing, gentle cleanser and patting dry. That is context-specific guidance; it does not set a bathing frequency for everyone.

The purpose here is to notice conditions that affect a plan: safe access, personal preferences, existing care advice and supplies. Do not override professional instructions or infer a skin diagnosis from an example.

## Worked example

Fictional Mei wants a bath but says stepping into the tub is difficult. A plan that only schedules a longer bath misses the access problem. Mei needs a suitable discussion about safe assistance or alternatives; this course cannot choose equipment or teach someone to lift Mei.

## Practise

Fictional Dara has written skin-care instructions from a clinician, but an online routine recommends different products. Write two questions Dara could ask before changing the routine.

## Suggested reasoning

Ask which parts of the existing instructions must be maintained and whether the proposed change is suitable for Dara's needs. Do not assume more products or hotter water improve care. The exercise cannot determine what the clinician would advise.

## Try a new situation

A towel and supplies are stored beyond a fictional person's reach. Suggest a planning change that can be discussed without asking them to attempt a risky reach. [Dry-skin reference](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/dry/dermatologists-tips-relieve-dry-skin).
