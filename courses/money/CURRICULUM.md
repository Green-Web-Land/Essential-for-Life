# Money Basics curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

**Audience:** adult beginners 18+. **Prerequisites:** none; basic arithmetic can be worked with a calculator or a supporter. **Materials:** course pages and paper or a local way to respond. No real finances required.

| Lesson | Goal | Evidence of practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Money and choices](lessons/01-choices.md) | Identify a goal, a trade-off and information still needed. | Goal, trade-off and missing fact. |
| [2. Income and payment timing](lessons/02-income.md) | Calculate take-home income from supplied figures and distinguish averages from cash received. | Net-pay calculation and timing distinction. |
| [3. Expenses and priorities](lessons/03-expenses.md) | Classify costs and allow for periodic bills without counting them twice. | Periodic allocation without double-counting. |
| [4. Build and revise a budget](lessons/04-budget.md) | Reconcile income with spending and planned transfers. | Reconciled budget and revision. |
| [5. Cash flow and surprises](lessons/05-cash-flow.md) | Find a timing gap even when a monthly total looks positive. | Identified lowest cash point. |
| [6. Saving, interest and purchasing power](lessons/06-saving.md) | Compare simple and compound calculations under explicit assumptions. | Interest comparison with assumptions. |
| [7. Accounts, payments and fees](lessons/07-fees.md) | Compare fictional fees for the same pattern of use. | Same-use fee comparison. |
| [8. Borrowing and total repayment](lessons/08-borrowing.md) | Compare borrowing costs without confusing a rate with total cost. | Total repayment and cost distinction. |

## Pacing

Plan about 25–40 minutes per lesson and 40–60 minutes for the final project, adjusted for reading, calculation and breaks. These are estimates, not tested durations. Speed is not a scoring criterion.

Read each explanation, attempt the exercise, compare reasoning and revise. Show units and assumptions. Use the [rubric](FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) for final-project review; all criteria can be revisited.

## Mathematical conventions

CAD throughout. A minus sign indicates a shortfall or modelled funding gap where stated. Financial examples exclude unlisted events only when explicitly specified. Interest rates are fictional, with the time basis stated. Retain calculation precision and round final displayed interest results to cents; real contracts may use different conventions.

A reserve transfer is not consumption. A loan receipt is not earned income. Budget totals and running account balances answer different questions.

## Limits

Complete introductory education, not a product-comparison service or personal financial plan. No required live calculator website, account, upload or transaction. No professional certification, validated test or demonstrated learner outcome. Future tax, investing or jurisdiction-specific modules would need separate scope and evidence.
