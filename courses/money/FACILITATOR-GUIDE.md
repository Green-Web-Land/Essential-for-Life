# Facilitator guide

[Course home](README.md) · [Curriculum](CURRICULUM.md)

## Prepare and support

Use fictional cards only. Never ask for pay slips, balances, credit reports, debts or personal spending histories. Allow learners to calculate with paper or a basic calculator, respond orally without recording, and take breaks.

Explain that the exercise is about checking assumptions and arithmetic. A shortfall is not a character judgement. Avoid assigning universal savings percentages or assuming essential expenses can be reduced. Offer the neutral fictional examples even if someone volunteers a personal story; the class is not a financial-advice session.

## Teaching prompts

Ask what each figure measures, which period it covers, whether it is confirmed and what is omitted. For an incorrect result, trace one operation at a time. Separate a computational correction from a personal recommendation.

Key distinctions to revisit: gross versus net; income versus borrowing; spending versus transfers; monthly remainder versus dated availability; interest rate versus all-in cost; low cost versus suitability.

## Final-project rubric

Use [Lesson 10](lessons/10-project.md). Mark Demonstrated or Revise, with reasons.

| Criterion | Demonstrated when the learner… |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly reconciliation | Gets CAD 2,800 income, CAD 2,550 spending/debt, CAD 150 reserve transfer, CAD 2,700 uses and CAD 100 unallocated. |
| Cash-flow check | Identifies the −CAD 225 day 8 gap and does not treat it as an authorised overdraft. |
| Transfers and closing amounts | Gets CAD 200 closing spending cash and CAD 350 combined cash, without double-counting the reserve or opening cash as income. |
| Borrowing comparison | Gets A cost CAD 48, B cost CAD 54 and explains the identical one-year assumptions and cost-only conclusion. |
| Uncertainty and help | Identifies missing resources/timing facts and an independently verified appropriate help route, without guaranteeing an adjustment. |
| Explanation | Shows operations/units and explains why a positive closing amount does not fund an earlier bill. |

All six are the instructional target, with revision allowed. This is an author-designed rubric, not a validated assessment or professional qualification.

## Answer review

Compare the supplied timeline in order; do not merely compare its ending balance. Ask the learner to explain where the CAD 150 reserve exists at the end. In the loan comparison, the full CAD 600 is received at the start and all charges are paid at maturity.

No live loan application or bank demonstration is required. If a real issue arises, distinguish general course discussion from appropriate qualified assistance.

## Adaptation

Retain [reuse notices](LICENSE.md) and label changes. Recompute every changed numerical example, including diagrams and answer guidance. New jurisdictions, child audiences or personalised tools need appropriate review. Alt text and a paper route do not establish formal accessibility compliance.
