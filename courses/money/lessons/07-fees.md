# 7. Accounts, payments and fees

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Learning goal:** Compare fictional fees for the same pattern of use.

An account's value depends on how it will be used, its terms and access needs. A low monthly fee may exclude transactions you need. Compare the same usage pattern, then consider service and accessibility, not cost alone.

These invented plans describe only counted transactions. All fees below are final CAD amounts, including any applicable taxes. No balance waiver, extra fee, interest or other feature applies in this exercise.

| Plan | Monthly fee | Included transactions | Each additional transaction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 4.00 | 10 | 0.50 |
| B | 8.00 | 30 | 0.50 |

For18 counted transactions, A costs CAD 8.00: CAD 4 plus8 × CAD 0.50. B also costs CAD 8.00. Equal fees do not prove equal suitability. At6 transactions, A costs CAD 4 and B costs CAD 8; unused included transactions do not produce a refund here.

A transfer moves money between accounts or recipients. A debit payment generally draws on account funds; a credit purchase creates an amount owed under the card agreement. A displayed credit limit is not income. Before a real payment, check recipient, amount, currency, fees and timing. Settlement and reversal possibilities depend on the method and provider.

This course does not send a payment or recommend a bank. The same comparison method can help identify questions without selecting a product for you.

## Worked example

Alex's fictional comparison uses the number of transactions that each plan actually counts. Counting all app taps as transactions would produce a misleading result. A real fee schedule would need checking.
