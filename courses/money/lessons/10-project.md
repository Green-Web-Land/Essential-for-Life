# 10. Final project: reconcile a household plan

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Learning goal:** Combine a budget, cash-flow calendar and a bounded cost comparison.

All amounts are fictional CAD for one month. Net income is CAD 1,400 on day 1 and CAD 1,400 on day 15. Opening spending-account cash is CAD 100, separate from income. Assume immediate availability, no holds, fees or transactions beyond the table. The reserve starts at zero in a separate account. A negative running amount represents a funding gap, not permission to overdraw.

| Day | Planned use | CAD |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2 | Rent | 1,300 |
| 5 | Utilities | 200 |
| 8 | Food, first portion | 225 |
| 18 | Food, second portion | 225 |
| 20 | Transport | 150 |
| 21 | Phone | 70 |
| 22 | Insurance | 100 |
| 25 | Existing debt payment | 180 |
| 26 | Personal/other spending | 100 |
| 28 | Transfer to reserve | 150 |

The existing debt payment is supplied as a cash use; its split is not calculated. The reserve transfer remains owned money. Separately compare offers A and B from lesson 8 using their exact assumptions. **Do not add a new loan to this month's table:** the comparison is a separate decision exercise, not an instruction to borrow.

Your answer should say what the figures establish and what remains unproven. A plausible adjustment needs verification; a paper change cannot guarantee permission to move a bill date.

## Worked example

After day 1 income and day 2 rent, the spending account has CAD 200. Paying day 5 utilities takes it to zero, before the next pay arrives. This is why a monthly total alone is not enough.

## Try it — paper is enough

Find total income, spending/debt payments, reserve transfer, total uses and unallocated remainder. Calculate running balances and the lowest point. Find closing spending cash and combined cash including the reserve. Compare A/B borrowing costs, then propose two questions before treating any change as workable.

## Answer and reasoning

Income CAD 2,800; spending/debt payments CAD 2,550; reserve transfer CAD 150; total uses CAD 2,700; unallocated remainder CAD 100. Running spending balances after opening, day 1,2,5,8,15,18,20,21,22,25,26,28 are:100;1,500;200;0;−225;1,175;950;800;730;630;450;350;200. Lowest is −CAD 225 on day 8. Closing spending cash is CAD 200; with CAD 150 reserved, combined cash is CAD 350. The unfunded interim gap still makes the original schedule unworkable as stated. A's cost is CAD 48 versus B's CAD 54, but neither is established as affordable. Ask whether timing can legitimately change and what verified resources/support exist before the gap.

Use the [rubric](../FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md), revise any double-counting and state why a positive closing balance does not resolve an earlier funding gap.
