# Developer reuse guide

[Course home](README.md)

Copy the entire course folder at an approved revision, with lessons, figures, guides, source notes and licence. No other course or live repository dependency is required.

## Preserve the learning material

Maintain relative links, headings, tables, answer guidance, image alt text and adjacent explanations. Keep fictional CAD labels and assumptions visible alongside every calculation. Do not display a number without its rate period, timing or relevant fee assumptions.

SVGs are editable originals; PNGs provide stable rendering. Neither contains executable scripts or external images. Diagrams simplify the supplied exercises rather than showing real bank interfaces.

## Numerical changes

Changing one input can affect the lesson, table, figure and final rubric. Recompute all affected outputs and review the reasoning. Do not repurpose the simple one-year balloon calculation as an instalment, credit-card, mortgage or APR calculator. Real financial products require their actual rules and suitable validation.

## Integration boundaries

The static files do not collect answers. Adding account connections, uploads, analytics, saving of financial records or personalised recommendations changes the reviewed scope and requires separate assessment. Retain an offline route.

Test links, rendering, keyboard access, text resizing and assistive-technology presentation in your implementation. The course author has not verified your renderer or claimed formal accessibility conformance.
