# Money Basics: Plan, Spend, Save and Borrow

Part of **Essential Knowledge for Life** · Adult beginners, 18+

Learn to organise a fictional budget, notice payment-timing gaps, compare stated costs and identify questions before making a decision. No other course is required.

## What you will practise

- Distinguishing gross/net income and estimates from confirmed receipts.
- Separating spending, transfers to reserves and unallocated cash.
- Checking a budget and the dates when money is needed.
- Comparing simple and compound interest under stated assumptions.
- Including fees and repayment timing in a cost comparison.
- Identifying reliable help and avoiding unsupported promises.

## Scope and assumptions

All amounts are fictional Canadian dollars (CAD), not typical living costs, current rates, product offers or forecasts. Examples specify their periods, fees and rounding. Correct arithmetic under supplied assumptions does not establish that a real choice is affordable, suitable or legally available.

This is general financial education, not personal investment, borrowing, tax or insolvency advice. Canadian sources are labelled; rules and services differ elsewhere. No personal circumstances or entitlements are assessed. A shortfall is information to investigate, not a judgement of someone's character.

The edition is written for adults 18+. This is an editorial audience choice, not tested suitability. Child-focused adaptations need their own review. No learner pilot, formal accessibility conformance or professional qualification is claimed. Static files do not collect learner answers; hosting providers have their own practices.
