## Arithmetic and scope

Every worked numerical example was recomputed by deterministic code against its stated inputs and expected results, including the final timeline and answer guidance. This is an author-side computational check, not independent professional assessment or a test of real products.

Fictional CAD amounts are not typical household costs, market rates or forecasts. Where rules could vary, the material stays at general concepts rather than inventing tax thresholds, debt remedies or eligibility. Canadian source context is explicit.

No bank connection, actual payment, application, credit check, online financial calculator, learner pilot or formal accessibility assessment was performed. A mathematical example cannot determine personal affordability or suitability. No professional qualification or guaranteed outcome is claimed.
