# Money Basics glossary

[Course home](README.md)

| Term | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| Affordability | Whether commitments can be met in the relevant circumstances; a low price alone does not establish it. |
| APR | Annual percentage rate, an annualised cost measure under applicable rules; do not equate our fee-excluding example rates with an all-in APR. |
| Balloon payment | A large payment due at a specified end date; our lesson 8 models repay everything once after one year. |
| Budget | A plan matching a period's receipts with spending and allocations. |
| CAD | Canadian dollars; all course amounts are fictional. |
| Cash flow | Money moving in and out over time. |
| Compound interest | Interest calculated on a balance that includes earlier interest under the stated compounding rule. |
| Credit | Borrowing or an arrangement to pay later under terms. |
| Debt | An amount owed. |
| Deduction | An amount taken from gross pay before net pay is received. |
| Fee | A charge for a service or transaction. |
| Fixed expense | A cost relatively stable over the chosen period, not necessarily essential. |
| Gross pay | Pay before deductions. |
| Income estimate | An expected amount, not a confirmed receipt. |
| Inflation | An increase in the cost of a basket of goods/services over time. |
| Interest | An amount charged or earned according to a rate and calculation rule. |
| Net pay | Pay after deductions. |
| Periodic expense | A cost due less often than the budget period. |
| Principal | The initial amount borrowed or deposited in the examples. |
| Purchasing power | What a money amount can buy. |
| Reserve | Money set aside for future use; availability and existing commitments matter. |
| Shortfall | Planned uses exceeding the relevant available amount. |
| Simple interest | Interest calculated on initial principal for the specified time. |
| Term | The duration of an arrangement. |
| Total repayment | All amounts paid back, including principal and stated borrowing charges. |
| Transfer | Moving money between accounts or recipients; a transfer between one's own accounts creates no new income. |
| Unallocated remainder | Money not yet assigned within the plan; not automatically disposable. |
| Variable expense | A cost that can change, not necessarily optional. |

These introductory meanings do not replace a real contract or applicable law. See [Sources](SOURCES.md).
