# Developer reuse guide

[Course home](README.md)

Copy the complete course folder at an approved revision, including all lessons, images, guides, sources and licence. It is static Markdown/SVG/PNG material and does not require a live repository dependency or another course.

## Preserve meaning and access

Keep relative links working, answer reasoning accessible and fictional labels prominent. Preserve image alt text, captions and adjacent text explanations. PNG files provide a stable rendering; SVG originals support editing. The SVGs contain local text and shapes, with no scripts or remote image dependencies.

Do not convert the exercises into real posting, login, upload or incident-report tasks without reviewing that changed scope. Required activities must retain their paper route. Preserve the option to withhold a post.

## Integration checks

Check every home/previous/next link and image in the actual interface. Test keyboard access, text resizing and assistive-technology presentation appropriate to the integration. The author has not tested your renderer or established formal accessibility compliance.

Static course files do not collect answers. If you add analytics, accounts, storage, recording or uploads, assess those features and their data practices separately. Their safeguards and legal requirements are not supplied by a paragraph in the course.

## Reuse and revision

Follow [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md), retain required notices, identify the source revision and describe changes. Do not imply original-publisher endorsement of an adaptation. Review platform-specific guidance before presenting it as current. A child-focused edition or a personalised advice service requires a separate review of the changed audience and function.
