# 10. Final project: a sharing decision

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Learning goal:** Prepare a supported, respectful draft and explain when you would withhold it.

Use only these fictional cards about an invented community repair afternoon. No real event, person, brand or account is represented. The dates are scenario details, not a live announcement.

**A — organiser notice, 1 May:** The repair afternoon is on 8 May at 13:00.

**B — organiser correction, 3 May:** The same event starts at 14:00 on 8 May. This replaces the earlier time. Beginners are welcome.

**C — forwarded picture:** Caption: “Today's packed workshop!” The supplied image-origin note says the picture is from last year's event. No photographer permission or reuse licence is supplied.

**D — Lee's message:** “You may mention that beginners are welcome. Please do not post my photograph or tell my personal story.”

**E — promotional offer:** An invented tool seller offers a free tool in exchange for a favourable post.

**F — group request:** “Please share C immediately and tell everyone it proves this year's event will be full.”

Your goal is to help a small group understand the current event information without misleading them or exposing someone. You may choose a text-only draft or decide to wait. Popularity is not a scoring criterion.

## Worked example

A useful start is: “The organiser's 3 May correction says 14:00 on 8 May; beginners are welcome (B).” It uses the current notice without claiming that every part of the event is known.

## Try it — paper is enough

Prepare: (1) an unsent factual draft with source; (2) a decision on C and F; (3) how you respect D; (4) your response to E and any disclosure issue; (5) a proposed audience and one copying limit; (6) a stopping point for your fictional session.

## Answer and reasoning

Use B and explain the corrected time. Do not use C to prove this year's attendance: its context and reuse rights are unresolved. Respect D without implying permission for the photo or story. Decline E, or pause promotion pending an honest, clearly disclosed and appropriately reviewed approach; no favourable claim is justified by a gift. Choose a limited audience while acknowledging copies can spread. Stop when the draft and open questions are recorded.

## Use it somewhere else

Review your answer with the rubric, revise any unsupported claim, then apply the method to a different invented announcement. Choosing not to share can pass when you explain the reasons.
