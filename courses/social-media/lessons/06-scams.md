# 6. Pause suspicious requests

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Learning goal:** Choose an independent verification route for an unexpected request.

A social-media message can impersonate a friend, employer, brand or support account. Warning signs include an unexpected demand, urgency and requests for credentials or money. Good spelling and a familiar profile picture do not prove legitimacy. A genuine account can also be compromised.

Separate recognising a risk from investigating a suspect link. You do not need to click it to decide to pause. Use contact information you already established independently, or the service's own known help route. Replying inside the suspicious conversation does not independently establish who is speaking.

Account protection includes using distinct strong credentials and additional authentication where supported. Multi-factor authentication adds an identity check but does not eliminate every threat. Do not provide sign-in codes, passwords or recovery codes to a person who asks for them. A security measure does not make every subsequent message trustworthy.

If a real account problem occurs, follow the actual provider's current recovery guidance. On a work-owned account or device, contact the organisation's established support channel. This course does not perform recovery, change settings or ask learners to disclose incidents.

For our paper exercise, the useful outcome is a safe next decision, not proving who committed a crime. Avoid publicly accusing a person when you only have a suspicious message.

## Worked example

A fictional account using Lee's photograph asks Sam to send a login code to “save the club page.” Sam does not send it. Sam contacts Lee using a previously established route and checks the service's known help page if needed.

## Try it — paper is enough

A fictional prize message requires a payment today and an identity photo. Write what you will avoid and how you would verify the claim without its link.

## Answer and reasoning

Do not pay, upload ID or open its attachment for this exercise. Check the supposed organiser through an independently established route. If no reliable confirmation exists, leave the claim unverified and decline the request.

## Use it somewhere else

Create a three-line paper reminder: requested action, independent check, smallest necessary disclosure.
