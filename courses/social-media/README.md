# Social Media: Use It with Purpose

Part of **Essential Knowledge for Life** · Adult beginners, 18+

Learn to choose a purpose, understand audiences, check posts and participate with care. Success can mean sharing a useful message, revising a draft or deciding not to post.

## Start learning

[Curriculum](CURRICULUM.md) · [First lesson](lessons/01-purpose.md)

No previous course is required. Every required activity works from these pages and paper; you may also record answers in a local document or discuss them without recording. No social-media account, installation, payment, real post, message or upload is needed.

## What you will practise

- Choosing a useful goal and a stopping point.
- Distinguishing a selected feed from evidence of public agreement.
- Considering audiences, copies, consent and identifying details.
- Checking sources and context without assuming visual AI detection is reliable.
- Pausing suspicious requests and choosing independent verification.
- Disagreeing respectfully and setting boundaries.
- Separating credit, reuse permission and promotional disclosure.

## Audience and evidence limits

Written for adults aged 18 and over. This is an editorial audience choice, not a tested suitability finding. Adaptations for children or teenagers need their own age, supervision and service-eligibility review.

Stories and source cards are fictional. Do not bring private experiences or real abuse material into the exercises. All required work can remain unsent and offline. Descriptions of services are documentation-based; features and reporting procedures vary. No device workflow, learner pilot or formal accessibility assessment has been performed.

The course provides general education, not individual legal or clinical advice, a professional qualification or a guarantee of safety. Canadian and Australian references are labelled by scope. Static files do not collect learner answers; hosting providers have their own practices.
