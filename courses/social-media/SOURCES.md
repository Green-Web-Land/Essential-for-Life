## Verification limits

Documentation was inspected; no live social-media account, feed experiment, posting, reporting, recovery or settings workflow was tested. Lessons 1 and 8 use original purpose/attention planning exercises without clinical outcome claims. No learner pilot, validated rubric or formal accessibility conformance assessment was performed.

Provider explanations can change and describe their own systems. The course's diagrams simplify concepts; they do not reveal proprietary weights or an individual user's profile. Uncertainty about a post's truth or origin remains uncertainty.

The course distinguishes general principles, service policy, law and actual controls. Legal requirements depend on activity and location; consult appropriate current guidance for a real commercial or high-stakes situation.
