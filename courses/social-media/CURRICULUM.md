# Social Media curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

**Audience:** adult beginners, 18+. **Prerequisites:** none. **Materials:** these pages and a way to respond, such as paper, a local document or unrecorded discussion. No account or social-media access required.

**Aim:** make reasoned decisions about attention, evidence, audiences and participation while retaining the choice not to post.

| Lesson | Learning goal | Practice evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Choose your purpose](lessons/01-purpose.md) | Choose a useful goal and an appropriate way to meet it. | A goal and alternative route. |
| [2. Understand a feed](lessons/02-feeds.md) | Explain why a feed is a selection rather than a complete picture. | A distinction between selection and public agreement. |
| [3. Know the audience and the limits](lessons/03-audiences.md) | Distinguish intended viewers from people who may receive a copy. | An intended audience and copying limit. |
| [4. Ask before sharing someone else's story](lessons/04-consent.md) | Make a sharing choice that respects a specific person's boundary. | A specific permission question. |
| [5. Check before repeating a claim](lessons/05-check-posts.md) | Write a supported conclusion while keeping uncertainty visible. | A supported correction with uncertainty. |
| [6. Pause suspicious requests](lessons/06-scams.md) | Choose an independent verification route for an unexpected request. | An independent verification route. |
| [7. Disagree and set boundaries](lessons/07-participation.md) | Choose a response that addresses an issue without escalating harm. | A respectful correction or boundary. |
| [8. Make an intentional attention plan](lessons/08-attention.md) | Design a small, adjustable routine around a chosen purpose. | An adjustable fictional attention plan. |
| [9. Create and credit responsibly](lessons/09-create-credit.md) | Separate factual support, reuse permission and promotional disclosure. | Separate checks for rights and promotion. |
| [10. Final project: a sharing decision](lessons/10-project.md) | Prepare a supported, respectful draft and explain when you would withhold it. | An unsent draft with justified decisions. |

## Pacing

Allow roughly 20–35 minutes per lesson and 30–50 minutes for the project, adjusted for reading, breaks and discussion. These are planning estimates, not tested completion times. No speed requirement applies.

Work the exercise before reading the suggested answer. Compare the reasons, revise and try the transfer task. Multiple answers may be defensible. A decision to withhold a post can be stronger than a confident but unsupported draft.

## Completion

Use the six-part [rubric](FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) for the final project. Aim to demonstrate all six, allowing revision. This is an author-designed learning check, not a validated test or qualification.

## Scope

No live experiments with recommendations, scams or harassment. No real messages, uploads, account changes or private learner logs. The course distinguishes principles, laws, platform policies and technical controls without claiming one worldwide rulebook. Live demonstrations or interactive integrations require their own appropriate review.
