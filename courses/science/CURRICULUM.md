# Becoming a Scientist — curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

Adult beginners 18+. No timed requirement. Read the explanations, attempt the supplied fictional activity, compare the reasoning and try the new situation. Paper, spoken answers or another accessible response format can be used; a calculator is optional.

| Lesson | Goal |
|---|---|
| [1. Science as inquiry](lessons/01-inquiry.md) | Explain how questions and evidence can change an explanation. |
| [2. Ask an investigable question](lessons/02-questions.md) | Turn a broad curiosity into a question linked to evidence. |
| [3. Observation, hypothesis and prediction](lessons/03-observations.md) | Keep a recorded observation separate from its explanation. |
| [4. Measurement and records](lessons/04-measurement.md) | Preserve units, method and uncertainty when recording a value. |
| [5. Fair comparisons and confounding](lessons/05-comparisons.md) | Identify what changes and what else could explain a difference. |

| [7. Check, repeat and correct](lessons/07-checking.md) | Distinguish rechecking an analysis from collecting new evidence. |
| [8. Research responsibly](lessons/08-responsibility.md) | Recognise integrity and permission questions before collecting data. |
| [9. Routes into science](lessons/09-pathways.md) | Build a realistic learning question about a scientific role. |
| [10. A complete investigation on paper](lessons/10-project.md) | Connect a claim to data, limitations and a better next check. |

## Learning route

Lessons 1–3 develop questions and distinguish evidence from explanation. Lessons 4–7 cover recording, comparisons, summaries and checks. Lessons 8–9 introduce responsibility and realistic learning pathways. Lesson 10 brings these together in one paper investigation.

Intended outcomes: explain a question, label a fictional observation, identify a confound, check simple arithmetic, preserve uncertainty and describe a responsible next step. These are learning goals, not validated outcomes or research competence certification.

No live experiment or real participant is part of the course. Physical proposals require a separate suitability, safety and permission assessment before anyone carries them out. Use only the supplied fiction.
