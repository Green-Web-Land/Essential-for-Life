# 9. Routes into science

[Course home](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Goal:** Build a realistic learning question about a scientific role.

Adults 18+. All named people, examples, role cards and datasets are fictional. Use paper or an accessible equivalent; a calculator is optional.

## Understand

Scientific work involves many kinds of contribution: framing questions, careful measurement, analysis, software, technical support and communication. The Government of Canada's careers page links separate resources for students and science professionals. It is a starting point for checking actual opportunities, not an offer or eligibility decision.

Requirements depend on the role, institution and jurisdiction. Some roles require specific education, supervised experience or credentials. Do not assume one degree is necessary for every contribution or that curiosity alone qualifies someone for restricted work.

Start with a field that interests you and a task you want to understand. Identify the skills involved, then consult a current official programme or role description for its requirements, costs and access options. This course supplies a fictional planning exercise; it does not require applying, paying or contacting an employer.

## Worked example

Fictional Sol likes organizing data. Sol lists a possible analysis-related role, the tasks it might involve and questions about mathematics, software and training. Sol marks every entry requirement unknown until checking a specific current source. The plan does not promise a job.

## Practise

Compare two invented role cards: A, “prepare data tables; training requirements not supplied”; B, “maintain laboratory instruments; supervised training required by this fictional employer.” Name one useful learning step and one unanswered question for each.

## Suggested reasoning

A: practise accurate tables with supplied data; ask which training the actual role requires. B: learn what instrument maintenance involves through approved educational material; ask about the required supervised route. Do not attempt maintenance or infer that this fictional condition applies to every employer.

## Try a new situation

A friend says this course makes you a certified scientist. Write a correction. Check: it teaches introductory reasoning; it awards no professional credential, admission or employment entitlement.
