# Becoming a Scientist: Ask, Test and Discover

Learn to ask useful questions, examine evidence and explain what remains uncertain. Explore scientific work without needing a laboratory.

**Adult beginners, 18+.** This is an editorial audience choice, not tested suitability. No previous course, scientific equipment, WebAI installation, AI account or purchase is required.

Read in order or choose a topic using the [curriculum](CURRICULUM.md). Each lesson includes a goal, explanation, fictional worked example, practice, suggested reasoning and a new situation.

All required activities use supplied fictional observations on paper or an accessible equivalent. A calculator is optional. No real experiment, participant recruitment, private-data collection or employer contact is required. A proposed investigation remains a paper plan; this course does not authorize it.

![Questions, evidence, interpretation and checking can feed back into one another.](images/inquiry.png)

Text equivalent: Ask a question, examine evidence, interpret it, and check the reasoning. Any part may lead back to an earlier question or method. This diagram is a simplified learning aid, not a universal sequence.

## What this course offers

Ten lessons and five diagrams introduce inquiry, measurement, fair comparisons, uncertainty, responsible research and routes into scientific work. The final exercise compares fictional records and tests the limits of competing claims.

Becoming a scientist takes learning and experience appropriate to the work. This course awards no professional credential, research permission, admission or employment guarantee. It does not teach hazardous investigations, statistical inference or how to obtain ethics approval.
