# Becoming a Scientist — developer guide

[Course home](README.md)

Copy the whole course directory with its lesson files, images, source notes and licence. Keep relative links intact or update and verify them when changing structure. Replace the parent-catalogue link appropriately if distributing the course alone.

## Preserve the learning model

Keep every dataset labelled fictional. Preserve units, caveats, original readings, calculation explanations and the distinction between a paper proposal and an actual experiment. Do not transform the examples into instructions to build equipment or collect human data.

Keep diagrams paired with their text equivalents and raw values. If you alter a value, update the worked answer, facilitator key, diagram and all affected claims, then recheck the arithmetic. Do not imply uncertainty intervals or statistical significance where only an observed range was calculated.

Retain the adult audience, optional participation, privacy boundaries and lack of professional certification. A child-facing version or added live experiment needs its own suitability assessment.

## Hosting and added features

These static files do not implement accounts, analytics or private research storage. A host application may add such behaviour; inspect and disclose its actual features separately. Do not infer that a host collects nothing from the static files alone.

AI, automated grading, participant studies and data collection are outside this package. Assess any addition before use. Do not present a generated answer, rubric score or internal publisher review as institutional research ethics approval.
