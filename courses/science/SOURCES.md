## Original teaching material

All people, labels, role cards, datasets and worked comparisons are authored fiction. Arithmetic examples teach sums, means and observed ranges. Their values are not measurements or evidence about actual surfaces, objects, people or scientific careers. No probability, confidence interval or causal effect is estimated.

The report structure and diagrams are editorial learning aids. The course does not claim that any one diagram describes all scientific practice.

## Uncertainty and maintenance

Retrieval gaps are recorded above. Included policy statements stay within the retrieved official passages; unresolved project-specific requirements are not guessed. This course gives no permission to conduct research and substitutes for no institution's review.

Recheck sources before materially changing guidance. No learner testing, independent scientific validation, professional legal certification or exhaustive jurisdictional review has been performed.
